The shopping trigger

Why do teams look for a Housecall Pro alternative?

Three reasons come up again and again: the per-user meter, the US-centric stack, and breadth that stops short of trade depth. Housecall Pro is genuinely good software — these are the places Canadian crews feel it strain.

  • Per-user fees compound — MAX is $329 USD/month and each extra user adds $35 USD. Every hire raises the software bill, and USD billing adds ~35% on top in CAD.
  • US-centric tax and payments — it's built around US sales tax and a US payments ecosystem. HST, recoverable input tax credits, and CRA-sensible subcontractor handling aren't the native grain of the product.
  • Home-service breadth over trade depth — dispatch and booking are excellent, but live per-job P&L, crew payroll ledgers with multiple pay models, and commission tracking are where NexusCRM goes deeper.

That last point matters most for plumbing and electrical & HVAC crews quoting real project work — not just flat-rate service calls.

Straight answers

Housecall Pro alternative — FAQ

Is NexusCRM a good Housecall Pro alternative for Canadian service businesses?

Yes — if you run a crew in Canada, NexusCRM removes the two things that make Housecall Pro expensive here: USD billing and per-user fees. You get flat CAD pricing with unlimited users, HST-native invoicing, all AI included, and a private dedicated instance with Canadian hosting available. If you depend on SMS and online card payments today, Housecall Pro still wins — both are on the NexusCRM roadmap.

How much does Housecall Pro really cost in Canada?

Housecall Pro's published prices are USD: Basic is $79/month, MAX is $329/month, and additional users are $35 USD each (public pricing pages, August 2026). At 2026 exchange rates, CAD costs run roughly 35% higher — MAX is about $444 CAD before extra seats. A 6-person crew paying for five seats beyond an included one lands near $504 USD, about $680 CAD/month; a 10-person crew near $644 USD, about $869 CAD. Exact totals depend on how many seats your plan includes. NexusCRM Pro is $425 CAD flat with unlimited users.

What does Housecall Pro do that NexusCRM doesn't yet?

Four things, honestly: SMS text messaging, online card payments, homeowner self-booking, and consumer financing. The first three are on the NexusCRM roadmap; financing is not. Housecall Pro also has a very polished consumer-facing experience and a cheap $79 USD Basic plan for solo operators. We would rather tell you this plainly than have you find out after you switch.

Does NexusCRM charge per user like Housecall Pro?

No. Every NexusCRM tier includes unlimited users — office staff, sales reps, crew leads, and field crews all log in at no extra cost, each with role-based permissions and a full audit trail. Housecall Pro charges $35 USD per additional user, so a growing crew raises your bill every time you hire; on NexusCRM the price stays flat as you grow.

Can NexusCRM import my Housecall Pro data?

Yes. Export your customers, price book, and job history from Housecall Pro as CSV, and our team migrates them into your private instance as part of implementation — included in every tier, with white-glove migration on Pro. Most businesses are live in about two weeks.

Does NexusCRM require a contract?

Yes — a 12-month initial term, then month-to-month, and annual prepay earns two months free. Housecall Pro is fairer here: it is month-to-month from day one. We ask for the term because every deployment is a private dedicated instance with real implementation work; on Pro, the setup fee can also be financed at $249/month over 24 months.
