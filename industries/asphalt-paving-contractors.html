Where does a paving company's season slip away?

Hot-mix plants typically shut down for the winter, so a whole year of revenue gets squeezed into a few busy months. Every unanswered enquiry, every tonne you under-quoted, and every scattered small job costs more when there's no off-season to make it back.

01

More enquiries than daylight

You're laying three driveways a day in June. Meanwhile the sealcoating requests, the pothole calls, and the "can you quote our lot?" emails pile up. By Thursday, the leads you meant to call Monday have booked someone else — and there's no November to catch up in.

02

Quoted 12 tonnes. Paid for 15.

The soft spot by the garage went deeper than it looked, the base needed more stone, and fuel went up mid-season. The plant tickets live in the truck's door pocket. Nobody lines them up against the quote until year end, when that "good" driveway turns out to have barely broken even.

03

One property manager, six lots, zero picture

Patching at one plaza, line painting at another, sealcoating at the third — each with its own invoice and its own follow-up. Add a dozen small residential seal jobs spread across the city, and you can't tell which clients or neighbourhoods are worth the drive.

AI in the field

What does voice-to-quote look like for a driveway?

Pace out the driveway, point at the cracked apron and the soft spot by the garage, and talk it through into your phone. By the time you're back at the truck, there's a priced draft quote with removal, base, tonnage, and tie-ins — based on the paving quotes you've already won.

  • Voice-to-quote — NexusCRM transcribes the walk, matches the customer, and drafts the lines in square feet and tonnes
  • Your rates, not a cost guide — prices come from your past paving quotes; any line below your historical low is flagged
  • AI Bid Advisor — margin checked against your price sheets and up to 25 similar completed jobs
  • Scope of work for the crew — AI drafts work, materials, and notes; print a versioned job sheet with signature lines

Nothing goes to the customer until you've reviewed and adjusted the draft.

Enquiry to paid invoice

What does a paving job look like on NexusCRM?

From the web form that came in at 9 a.m. to the final roll of the compactor, one system holds the job — and the margin is visible the whole way.

A driveway enquiry lands from your website or Meta ad, and the AI Caller phones the homeowner while they're still comparing pavers. Size, condition, and timeline are on the lead before your estimator's first coffee.

Acceptance locks a signed snapshot with timestamp and IP, then creates the invoice, the project, and the schedule entry automatically. Still unsigned? Expiry reminders go out, and the AI Caller can follow up by phone.

The excavation task stays Blocked until your Ontario One Call locates are back. Removal and paving are booked as separate visits; if the forecast turns, edit the visit in place and the customer gets a rescheduled email automatically.

Plant tickets hit the P&L the same day

The foreman photographs the tickets, logs the crew's shift in one tap, and the tonnage, fuel, and labour land on the live job P&L — so an overrun shows up while you can still price the next driveway right.

Paid in full — and ready for next year

After photos go to the customer portal, final payment emails the receipt, and next season's sealcoat quote is one duplication away.

Paving contractor CRM questions, answered

How much does CRM software for asphalt paving contractors cost in Canada?

NexusCRM is flat-priced in CAD with unlimited users: Core is $295/month plus $2,950 one-time implementation, Pro is $425/month plus $5,500 (the fit for most paving companies running their own crews and trucks), and Elite is $795/month plus $9,800. For comparison, Jobber Grow runs $199–$499 plus $29 per extra user, Housecall Pro MAX runs $329 plus $35 per extra user, and ServiceTitan runs $245–$500 per technician per month plus $5,000–$15,000 implementation (public pricing pages and verified user reports, August 2026). Because users are unlimited, the extra labourers you bring on for the season don't add to the bill. The AI Caller for inbound, outbound and follow-up calls is an optional add-on from $297/month.

Can NexusCRM quote paving jobs by square footage and tonnage?

Yes. Every quote is built from line items with quantities and auto-totals, so a driveway can carry removal and haul-away by the square foot, compacted granular base, surface asphalt by the tonne, and edging and tie-ins as separate lines. Multi-option quotes let you price a resurface over the existing base next to a full removal and rebuild, and the customer ticks the one they want on their phone and signs. Voice-to-quote drafts those lines from your own past paving quotes and flags any line priced under your historical low.

How do I track plant tickets and fuel costs against each paving job?

Photograph them. Receipt capture uses AI vision to read the vendor, date, and line items off a plant ticket or fuel receipt and turn them into itemized expenses on that job, linked to the image. Labour is computed automatically from logged crew hours at locked pay rates, recoverable HST is tracked separately, and the live job P&L shows the real margin while the season is still running — so a driveway that took three more tonnes than you quoted shows up that week, not at year end.

Can NexusCRM handle property managers with several commercial lots?

Yes. Each lot runs as its own project with its own quote, invoice, and schedule, and project groups roll related projects together so you see the combined profitability of that client's work — patching at one plaza, line painting at another, sealcoating at a third. The geographic profitability map pins every job by margin with postal-area rollups, so you can see which parts of the city your crews actually make money in.

Does NexusCRM support repeat sealcoating customers?

Partly today, and we'd rather say so plainly: recurring jobs are on the NexusCRM roadmap. Today, quote duplication with lineage turns last year's sealcoat into this year's quote in two clicks, "pause until spring" parks late-fall enquiries until you're paving again, and bulk promo campaigns let you send a sign-this-month discount across the open quotes you select, with the discount expiring automatically.

Can the AI Caller keep up with calls during paving season?

Yes. The AI Caller is a NexusAI add-on, from $297/month, built on NLPearl voice agents and wired to NexusCRM through n8n. It answers your line 24/7 and asks what your estimator would: new paving, resurfacing, sealcoating, crack filling or patching; residential or commercial; rough size and condition; and timeline. When the call ends, the lead lands in NexusCRM with a summary and transcript, tagged AI Caller. It also phones new web-form and Meta leads within minutes and follows up on quotes that haven't been accepted. It identifies itself as an AI assistant, transfers callers who want a person, and only calls people who contacted you or asked for a quote.
