Built for this trade first

How does a CRM built by landscapers fix it?

By speaking your trade's language natively — square feet of interlock, linear feet of wall, loads of granular A — instead of making you translate your business into generic software fields.

  • Voice-to-quote for interlock, walls, and full builds — record the site walk on your phone; line items and prices are drafted from your own quote history
  • AI Bid Advisor — sanity-checks your $/sq ft and $/lin ft against your price sheets and up to 25 similar completed jobs before you hit send
  • Per-linear-foot sub pay — pay wall crews by the lin ft with a running ledger and self-serve "what am I owed"
  • Crew job sheets on mobile — scope, site photos, gate codes, and materials on their phones; no laptop in the truck
  • Photo documentation customers love — before, during, and after shots land in the customer portal automatically
  • Seasonal pipeline — quote all winter, stack spring starts, and let follow-up automation rebook last year's clients
  • Live job P&L — stone, aggregate loads, bin rentals, and fuel receipts photographed straight onto the job that spent them

A build season, run properly

What does a day on NexusCRM look like?

The same day you're having now — minus the 9 p.m. spreadsheet, the lost receipts, and the "are we still on for Tuesday?" calls.

7:05 — Crews open their job sheets

Today's scope, site photos, materials, and access notes on their phones. Nobody calls the office — the office is your truck.

9:30 — You record a site walk

Two minutes talking through the dig-out, 12" base, herringbone interlock, and a two-course garden wall. The draft quote is priced before you leave the driveway.

11:00 — Bid Advisor flags the wall line

It's under your usual $/lin ft. You bump it and send the quote with two options — standard pavers or premium slab.

2:15 — Customer taps "Accept"

Invoice, project, schedule slot, and crew notifications are created automatically. The signed quote snapshot is locked with timestamp and IP.

5:40 — Receipt photographed from the cab

The stone-yard receipt lands on the job's P&L, itemized by AI. You know the real margin in June — not November.

Landscaping CRM questions, answered

How much does a CRM for landscapers and hardscape contractors cost in Canada?

NexusCRM costs a flat $295–$795/month CAD with unlimited users — most landscaping companies choose Pro at $425/month plus a $5,500 one-time implementation, with every AI feature included. A six-person landscape crew pays NexusCRM one flat rate; on per-user platforms the meter runs all season. Details on the pricing page.

Was NexusCRM really built inside a landscaping company?

Yes. NexusCRM was built inside a real GTA contracting company whose bread and butter is interlock, retaining walls, and landscape construction — and it ran that company's daily operations through full seasons before it was ever offered for sale. The AI's draft quotes were validated line by line against real base, sand, and stone quantities per square foot on live jobs. It is landscaping business software in Canada that started as a landscaper's own tool, not a boardroom product.

Can the AI actually price interlock and retaining wall jobs?

Yes — because it prices from your numbers, not generic guesses. Record a site walk on your phone and NexusCRM drafts a quote with line items grounded in your own historical quotes. The AI Bid Advisor then checks the margin against your price sheets and up to 25 similar completed jobs — so a driveway quoted light on base depth or a wall priced under your usual per-linear-foot rate gets flagged before you hit send. You review and approve every quote; nothing goes out on its own.

Can I pay a wall crew by the linear foot?

Yes. NexusCRM supports four crew pay models, including per-unit pay — so a wall crew can be paid by the linear foot, an interlock sub by the square foot, and an hourly labourer by the hour, all on the same job. Crew and subcontractor ledgers track what has been earned and paid, crews can self-serve check what they are owed, and every pay line lands on that job's live P&L so you see true margin, not just revenue.

Does NexusCRM handle the seasonal side of landscaping?

Yes — the pipeline is built for a seasonal trade. Quote all winter and stack spring starts, let follow-up automation rebook last year's clients before the phones go quiet, and see which lead sources actually filled your build season with source ROI analytics. Photo documentation from finished builds feeds next season's sales. Recurring-visit scheduling for weekly maintenance and snow routes is on the roadmap; today those are handled as scheduled jobs you control.

Can NexusCRM text customers or take payments on-site?

SMS text messaging and online card payments are both on the roadmap, along with QuickBooks Online sync. Today, customers accept quotes online with one tap on a branded quote page (immutable signed snapshots with timestamp and IP), get automatic email updates with open and click tracking, and see project status, photos, and invoices in their own customer portal. Invoices support payment schedules, and HST is handled natively.
