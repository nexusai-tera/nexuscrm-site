Sound familiar?

Where does a concrete job's margin disappear?

It goes to pours that move three times, truck charges nobody put on the quote, and crack callbacks with no signed terms to point to. Concrete gives you one shot on pour day, and the admin around it is where money leaks.

01

Pour day, rained out

Thursday's forecast turns overnight. Now the ready-mix order, the pump, three finishers, and the homeowner all have to move. You rebook from the truck at 6 a.m., and the calendar on the crew's phones still says Thursday. Two guys show up to the wrong driveway.

02

The truck charges you never quoted

The walkway turned into a small top-up load with a short-load fee. The backyard patio needed a pump nobody priced. The dig-out went deeper, so the base took more stone. Each charge is small, and together they eat the margin on a job you thought was solid.

03

The October crack call

Four months after the pour, the homeowner phones about a hairline crack and calls it a defect. Your crack terms were a verbal "concrete cracks, that's why we cut joints." There are no signed terms, no photos of the joints, and no record of the care instructions you sent.

How NexusCRM fixes it

What makes this job tracking software for concrete contractors?

It tracks what a concrete job actually runs on: square footage, finish choices, prep/pour/seal visits that depend on weather, and supplier and pump charges that pile up by the load. That holds for a 120-square-foot shed pad or a stamped pool deck.

AI in the field

What does voice-to-quote look like for concrete?

Pace out the old patio, talk through the square footage, the break-out, the base, the finish, and the sealer. The draft quote is priced before you've rolled up the tape. It's built from your own past concrete quotes, not a national cost guide.

  • Voice-to-quote: record the walk, and NexusCRM transcribes it, matches the customer, and drafts square-foot line items
  • Finish options on one quote: add broom and exposed aggregate next to stamped, so the homeowner picks
  • AI Bid Advisor: checks your margin against price sheets and up to 25 similar completed jobs before you send
  • Flags thin pricing: any line priced under your historical low gets a warning

Can one quote show broom, exposed aggregate and stamped finishes?

Yes. A NexusCRM quote can carry Good, Better and Best options, so the same 600-square-foot driveway can be priced as broom finish, exposed aggregate and stamped, with break-out and removal, base prep, forming and sealing as separate lines. The homeowner opens the quote on their phone, ticks the finish they want with a live running total, and signs with a typed signature; the accepted snapshot is locked with IP address and timestamp, and the invoice bills exactly the option they chose.

What happens in NexusCRM when rain moves a pour?

You re-time the pour-day visit in place. NexusCRM patches the same Google Calendar event instead of creating a duplicate, the change is pushed within about a minute, and the customer gets the automatic rescheduled email. Prep, pour and seal can be booked as separate visits on one job, so moving the pour does not disturb the rest of the schedule, and the day-before 6 PM reminder goes to the crew for whichever day the pour lands on. If a job is waiting on weather, you can put it On Hold with a required reason so the whole office sees why.

How do I track ready-mix, pump and short-load costs per job?

Log them as job expenses the day they happen. Photograph the ready-mix invoice, the pump truck bill or the short-load charge and AI receipt capture reads the vendor, date and line items into expenses attached to the photo. Crew labour is calculated from logged hours at the pay rate locked when the crew was assigned, so the live job P&L shows revenue, labour, materials and recoverable HST together, and the at-risk watchlist flags low-margin jobs while they are still open rather than at year end.

Can NexusCRM help with crack complaints and warranty disputes?

It gives you the paper trail. Write your warranty terms, including how you handle hairline and shrinkage cracks, into NexusCRM's warranty library once, and they are snapshot-copied onto every quote, so the terms the customer signed never change after the fact. Job photos can be tagged Before, In Progress and After, and every email the system sent the customer, from scheduling to completion, is recorded on the project with its delivery status.
