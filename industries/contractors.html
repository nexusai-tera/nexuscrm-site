Where does a renovation's profit actually go?

It leaks — through unpriced change orders, sub sequencing chaos, and costs nobody tallies until tax season. Multi-week renos and builds punish loose tracking harder than any service call ever will.

01

The margin you discover in March

The kitchen wrapped in October. Sub invoices trickled in through November. The lumber receipts lived in the truck. Your accountant tells you in March that the job made 9% — or lost money. By then there's nothing you can do about it.

02

Change orders that never got priced

"While you're in the ceiling, can you add three pot lights?" Said on site, done that afternoon, never written up, never signed. A dozen of those across a basement build and your contingency is gone — and you can't prove any of it was extra.

03

Sub sequencing by group chat

The insulator is booked before electrical rough-in passes inspection. The tile guy shows up to wet compound. Every slip costs a reschedule, and the whole sequence lives on a whiteboard and three text threads only you can decode.

Annual prepay earns two months free, and financed implementation is available. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Can NexusCRM handle deposits and progress-draw payment schedules?

Yes. Invoices carry payment schedules, so a $55,000 basement gets a deposit plus draws tied to milestones — rough-in, drywall, completion — instead of one invoice at the end. Every payment is recorded against the schedule, the customer sees paid and due status in their portal, and the job P&L reconciles what has been collected against what is still owed. HST is handled natively on every draw.

How does NexusCRM track profit on a multi-week renovation?

Every job carries a live P&L that updates as the work happens: crew hours costed under four different pay models, materials and subcontractor invoices logged against the job, receipts photographed on-site and itemized by AI, and recoverable HST tracked separately. You see the real margin mid-job — while there is still time to act on it — not months after closing when the accountant reconciles the year.

Can it handle change orders without losing money?

Yes. When the client asks for three more pot lights or a bigger island, you revise the quote, price the change, and send it for one-tap online acceptance from their phone. The accepted version is locked as an immutable signed snapshot with timestamp and IP, and the full revision history shows exactly what changed and when. Scope creep gets priced and signed instead of quietly absorbed.

Can it schedule subcontractors and multiple crews?

Yes. The crew calendar syncs to multiple Google Calendar accounts through a background worker, day-before reminders go out automatically, and field crews get mobile PWA views with the scope, photos, and documents for each job. Crew and subcontractor ledgers track what each person and sub is owed, with four pay models and self-serve balance lookup. A GPS time clock is on the roadmap.

Does NexusCRM integrate with QuickBooks for my bookkeeper?

QuickBooks Online sync is on the roadmap, alongside SMS and online card payments. Today, NexusCRM gives your bookkeeper clean CSV exports, HST-native tracking including recoverable HST on job costs, payment schedules on invoices, and webhook integrations with Zapier and n8n. Every change is covered by a before/after audit log.
