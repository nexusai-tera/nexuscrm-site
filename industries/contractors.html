Contractor editions

Which contractor edition fits your business?

Each edition speaks its trade's language: linear feet of fence, square feet of pavers, cubic metres of ready-mix, tonnes of asphalt, draws on a renovation, and subs on a multi-trade build.

01 · GENERAL CONTRACTORS

General Contractors

Price multi-trade bids with the AI Bid Advisor and sequence subs around inspections. Bill progress draws with holdback on its own milestone, and see every sub balance and job margin live.

 02 · RENOVATION

Renovation Contractors

Quote kitchens, bathrooms and basements with builder-grade to premium finish options. Deposits and draws, signed change orders, and a homeowner portal that cuts "where's my job?" calls.

 03 · FENCING

Fencing Contractors

Quote pressure-treated, cedar and vinyl by the linear foot. Split shared back lines between neighbours with project groups, and pay install crews per foot.

 04 · INTERLOCK

Interlock Contractors

Price driveways, patios, steps and re-levelling by the square foot from your own past quotes, and send crews job sheets with base tonnage and poly sand.

 05 · CONCRETE

Concrete Contractors

Put broom, exposed aggregate and stamped finishes on one quote. Move rained-out pours without duplicate calendar events, and see ready-mix and pump charges on the live job P&L.

 06 · ASPHALT & PAVING

Asphalt & Paving Contractors

Quote driveways and parking lots by the square foot and the tonne, photograph plant tickets onto the job P&L, and reschedule crews when rain moves in.

Landscaping and hardscape crews have their own edition: CRM for landscapers.

Contractor CRM questions, answered

Which contractor trades does NexusCRM have editions for?

Six: general contractors, renovation contractors, fencing contractors, interlock contractors, concrete contractors, and asphalt paving contractors. Landscapers and hardscapers have their own edition too. Every edition runs on the same platform, tuned during implementation to the trade's units, price book, quote templates, crew pay models, and pipeline stages.

How much does a CRM for contractors cost in Canada?

NexusCRM is flat-priced in CAD with unlimited users: Core is $295/month plus $2,950 one-time implementation, Pro is $425/month plus $5,500 (the fit for most contracting companies), and Elite is $795/month plus $9,800. For comparison, Buildertrend typically runs $666–$833/month, ServiceTitan runs $245–$500 per technician per month plus $5,000–$15,000 implementation, and JobNimbus runs $225–$550 base plus $25–$75 per user (public pricing pages and verified user reports, August 2026). Annual prepay earns two months free, and financed implementation is available. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Is each contractor edition a different product?

No. It's one platform, so every edition gets the full pipeline: lead intake, AI voice-to-quote, online e-acceptance, automatic invoices and projects, crew scheduling with Google Calendar sync, live job P&L, and payroll. What changes is the setup. A fencing company quotes by the linear foot, an interlock company by the square foot, and a general contractor by trade and phase, and the AI drafts every quote from that company's own past quotes.

Can NexusCRM answer the phone for my contracting business?

Yes, with the AI Caller add-on. Built on NLPearl voice agents and wired into NexusCRM with n8n, it answers inbound calls 24/7 and qualifies callers in your trade's terms. It phones new web and Meta Lead Ads leads within minutes, follows up on quotes that haven't been accepted, and logs a summary and transcript on the lead after every call. Plans start at $297/month CAD. See the AI Caller page.

How long does it take a contracting company to go live?

About two weeks for most contracting companies. Implementation covers your dedicated private instance, migrating clients and your price book, branding quotes, invoices and the customer portal, connecting Google Calendar and lead sources, and training office staff and field crews.
