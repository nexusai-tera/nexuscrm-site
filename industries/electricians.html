Between the quote backlog, inspection-day chaos, and receipts riding around in the dash of the van. Service calls keep the lights on — installs and retrofits make the money, and that's exactly where the paperwork breaks down.

Electrician & HVAC CRM questions

How much does a CRM for electricians and HVAC contractors cost in Canada?

NexusCRM is flat-priced in CAD with unlimited users: Core is $295/month plus $2,950 one-time implementation, Pro is $425/month plus $5,500, and Elite is $795/month plus $9,800. Annual prepay earns 2 months free. Compare that to per-user platforms: a 6-person shop on Jobber Grow pays $199–$499+ base plus $29 per extra user, Housecall Pro MAX runs $329 plus $35 per user, and ServiceTitan runs $245–$500 per technician per month with a $5,000–$15,000 implementation. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Can one system handle service calls, installs, and panel upgrade projects?

Yes. A $340 troubleshooting call and a multi-day 200A service upgrade or heat-pump retrofit live in the same pipeline but flow differently: quick tickets get quoted and invoiced same-day, while projects get multi-option quotes, milestone scheduling across days, crew assignments, and a live per-job P&L. Lead-source analytics tell you whether the EV-charger ads or the no-heat calls are actually paying.

Does NexusCRM handle HVAC maintenance contracts and recurring service?

Recurring jobs are on the roadmap and shipping soon. Today, you can track maintenance customers with automated follow-up reminders, book seasonal tune-ups on the shared calendar with Google Calendar sync, and keep every furnace, AC, and heat-pump install's full history on the customer record — so when recurring jobs land, your data is already in place.

How does voice-to-quote work for a panel upgrade or heat-pump install?

Record the site walk on your phone — panel location, mast condition, run lengths, tonnage, ductwork notes. NexusCRM transcribes it, matches the customer, and drafts a priced quote with line items grounded in your own historical quotes — your panel-upgrade prices, not generic book rates. You review and adjust before sending, and the AI Bid Advisor checks the margin against your price sheets and up to 25 similar completed jobs.

Can it keep ESA inspection days under control?

NexusCRM doesn't file your ESA permits for you, but it keeps inspection days from colliding with everything else: rough-in and final inspection dates sit on the job schedule, sync to every licensed tech's Google Calendar across multiple accounts, and automated day-before reminders go out so the customer is actually home when the inspector arrives.

Do we pay per user for every apprentice and office admin?

No. Every tier includes unlimited users — journeypersons, apprentices, office staff, and subcontractors all get logins with role-based access across 4 roles. On per-user platforms a growing crew means a growing bill; NexusCRM stays flat. Your data also lives on a dedicated private instance with its own database, with Canadian hosting available.
