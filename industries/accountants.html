Why do accounting firms need a CRM?

Because the chasing — not the accounting — is what buries the office from February to April. Your tax suite prepares returns; nothing you run today manages the 300 client relationships feeding it.

◷

The March slip chase

Forty email threads titled "RE: RE: missing T5." The client who swears they sent the RRSP receipt. The T4 that arrives as a sideways photo the night before the deadline — and no way to see who even opened your reminder.

☏

"Is my return done yet?"

Every status call pulls a preparer off a file for ten minutes. Multiply by tax season. Clients aren't being difficult — they just have no way to see where their T1 or year-end sits without phoning the office.

▤

Intake on sticky notes

A referral from a client's dinner party lands in a partner's inbox in January and surfaces in April. New-business inquiries, unsent engagement letters, unbilled work — tracked in a spreadsheet nobody updates after February 15.

The fix

How does NexusCRM work for an accounting practice?

NexusCRM runs the client side of the practice — intake, engagement letters, document collection, follow-up, and billing — as pipelines and projects your whole team can see. It's client management software for accountants: the workflow layer in front of your tax suite, not a replacement for it.

Intake pipeline
  • Website forms and referrals flow straight into a new-client pipeline
  • Statuses from Inquiry → Proposal Sent → Engaged, with auto-transitions
  • Follow-up reminders so January referrals don't surface in April
  • Source tracking — see which referral partners actually send business
Engagements as projects
  • T1 season batches, T2 year-ends, monthly bookkeeping, HST filings, payroll runs
  • Task checklists per engagement: docs in → prep → review → e-file → invoice
  • Pipeline view of every open file — partners see status without asking
  • Deadlines and assignments visible to the whole team, not one inbox
Document collection
  • One secure portal link per client: status, documents, invoices
  • Clients see exactly which slips are outstanding — without calling
  • T4s, T5s, donation receipts, and statements in one place per engagement
  • Internal notes and costs structurally can't leak to the client view
Slip-chasing automation
  • Scheduled email chasers for missing documents — set once, stop nagging manually
  • Open and click visibility: know who ignored you vs. who never saw it
  • Branded emails from your firm, logged on the client record
  • Day-before deadline reminders go out automatically
Engagement letters
  • Send letters as quotes with one-tap online acceptance
  • Immutable signed snapshot locked with timestamp and IP
  • Acceptance auto-creates the invoice and the engagement project
  • Multi-option proposals — compliance-only vs. full-service tiers
Billing
  • Invoices created from accepted engagements — no retyping
  • HST handled natively; payment schedules for monthly retainers
  • CSV exports keep your own ledgers current (QuickBooks sync on the roadmap)
  • Rep commissions and referral tracking if you pay for business development

Honesty note: NexusCRM is not bookkeeping or tax-prep software — you'll still use your tax suite and QuickBooks/Xero. Field-service modules like job costing and crew scheduling are simply hidden for accounting firms. See all features.

AI that does the work

What does the AI do for accountants?

Dictate a two-minute debrief after a discovery call and NexusCRM drafts a priced engagement letter — grounded in your firm's own historical fees, not generic guesses.

  • Voice-to-engagement — talk through what the prospect needs; get a draft proposal with line items and fees
  • AI Bid Advisor — checks proposed fees against your rate card and up to 25 similar completed engagements
  • Receipt capture — photograph a working lunch or software receipt; AI itemizes it as a firm expense
  • AI assistant — "which T1 clients still owe us slips?" — around 73 permission-aware tools, every action confirmed and logged

Every AI action runs with your confirmation, cost caps, and a full before/after audit trail — the kind of history an accountant appreciates.

Zero double entry

What does tax season look like on NexusCRM?

Calmer — the intake, chasing, and status calls run themselves, so preparers prepare and partners review. Here's the flow for one new client:

Inquiry lands in the intake pipeline

A referral or website form creates the lead with source attribution. Follow-up reminders start on schedule — no more January referrals surfacing in April.

Engagement letter goes out with e-acceptance

The client opens a branded proposal on their phone and accepts with a tap. The signed snapshot is locked with timestamp and IP.

Portal opens with a document checklist

Acceptance auto-creates the invoice and the engagement project, and the client gets their portal link — they can see what's outstanding without phoning.

Chasers go after the missing slips

Automated emails follow up on the T5 that never arrived, with open/click visibility so you know who to actually call.

Partners watch every file live

Docs in → prep → review → e-file → invoiced, across all 300 files at once. Deadlines sync to Google Calendar across the whole team.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a CRM for accountants and bookkeeping firms cost in Canada?

For most accounting and bookkeeping firms, NexusCRM Core is the right fit: a flat $295/month CAD plus a $2,950 one-time implementation, with unlimited users — partners, preparers, bookkeepers, admin, and seasonal staff all included. All AI features are included, annual prepay earns 2 months free, the initial term is 12 months then month-to-month, and financed implementation is available. Compare that with per-user platforms like Salesforce, where AI alone is a $125 per user per month add-on. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Is NexusCRM bookkeeping or tax software?

No — and we're direct about that. NexusCRM is the client-relationship and workflow layer that sits in front of your tax and bookkeeping tools: intake, engagement tracking, document collection, follow-up, engagement letters, and billing. You still prepare returns in your tax suite and keep ledgers in QuickBooks or Xero. NexusCRM makes sure the clients, documents, and deadlines feeding those tools never fall through the cracks.

Does NexusCRM sync with QuickBooks or Xero?

Direct QuickBooks Online sync is on the roadmap. Today, NexusCRM gives you clean CSV exports of invoices, payments, and expenses so your own ledgers stay current, plus webhook integrations with Zapier and n8n for custom pipelines. Email automation with open and click tracking works out of the box.

Can clients check status and send documents without calling the office?

Yes. Every client gets one private portal link showing engagement status, documents, and invoices — so "is my return done yet?" gets answered by the portal, not by a preparer picking up the phone in March. Internal notes and costs structurally cannot leak to the client view.

Can we use NexusCRM for engagement letters?

Yes. Send an engagement letter as a quote with online acceptance: the client opens a clean branded page on their phone and accepts with a tap. NexusCRM locks an immutable signed snapshot with timestamp and IP, and acceptance can automatically create the invoice and the engagement project — no retyping.

Is client data private and hosted in Canada?

Yes. Every firm gets a dedicated private instance with its own database — client data is never pooled with other companies — and Canadian hosting is available, supporting PIPEDA-aligned data-residency expectations your clients ask about. Four-role access control, soft delete with restore, and a before/after audit log on every change round it out.
