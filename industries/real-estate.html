The listings you lose

Sound familiar?

Most agents don't lose deals on negotiation — they lose them in the days nobody followed up. The best CRM for real estate agents in Canada isn't the one with the most features; it's the one that makes sure the next touch actually happens, for you and for every agent on your team.

01

Speed-to-lead is a coin flip

The buyer lead from your Bloor West listing ad lands while you're mid-showing. Six hours later you reply — and they've already booked with whoever answered first. You never find out which ads produced closings and which just produced clicks.

02

Follow-up lives on sticky notes

The couple who said "call us in the spring." The open-house sign-ins from three Sundays ago. Your 2023 buyers, now two years from their next move, who'd refer you tomorrow — if anyone checked in. No system is counting, so nobody calls.

03

Closing-day math in a spreadsheet

Co-listing splits, an out-of-town referral fee, the team-lead override — reconciled at 11 p.m. against the trade record. Every agent on the team quietly keeps their own tally of what they're owed, and the tallies never match.

Day in the life

What does a listing week look like on NexusCRM?

A week where nothing falls through — because nothing depends on your memory.

Monday — the lead lands itself

Your Meta ad for the High Park semi produces four buyer leads overnight. Each lands in the pipeline with its source attached, and the intro cadence starts before your first coffee.

Tuesday — the listing pitch goes out

You send the seller your listing-services package from NexusCRM. They open it on their phone, accept with a tap, and the signed snapshot is locked with timestamp and IP.

Wednesday — showings book themselves onto the calendar

Three showings and an evening listing appointment go in as meetings, sync to every team member's Google Calendar, and day-before reminders go out automatically.

Thursday — the voice memo does the paperwork

After each showing you record a one-minute memo. Notes are logged against the client, and the follow-up — comps emails, second showings, offer-night reminders — is on the schedule.

Friday — the split is already recorded

The Danforth deal firms up. The co-listing split, the referral fee, and the team-lead override are on the deal record — every agent can see what they're owed without asking.

Real estate CRM questions, answered

How much does a CRM for real estate agents and teams cost in Canada?

NexusCRM Core is a flat $295/month CAD plus a $2,950 one-time implementation — the right fit for a solo agent. Teams run Pro at $425/month plus $5,500 implementation, with unlimited agents and staff on both tiers and every AI feature included. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Does NexusCRM integrate with MLS or TRREB?

No — NexusCRM does not connect to MLS, TRREB, or other board systems, and we won't pretend otherwise. It's the relationship and follow-up engine that runs alongside your board and brokerage tools: it owns lead capture, follow-up cadences, showing schedules, client communication, and commission tracking — the work that actually wins and keeps clients.

Can NexusCRM track commission splits for my team?

Yes. NexusCRM tracks per-agent commissions with configurable splits, keeps a running ledger per team member, and gives every agent a self-serve view of what they're owed. Co-listings, referral fees, and team-lead overrides are recorded against the deal instead of reconciled in a spreadsheet the night before payday.

How does the AI help after a showing?

Record a voice memo in your car after the showing. NexusCRM transcribes it, matches the client, logs structured notes — what they loved, their concerns, their budget — and schedules the follow-up: the comparables email, the second-showing hold on your Google Calendar, the offer-deadline reminder. Every AI action runs with your confirmation and lands in the audit trail.

Can NexusCRM text my leads?

SMS text messaging is on the active roadmap, alongside online card payments and QuickBooks sync. Today, follow-up runs on automated email with open and click tracking — you can see the moment a buyer opens your comparables email — plus scheduled reminders so calls happen on time, every time.

How long does setup take for a real estate team?

Most teams are live in about two weeks. Implementation covers your private instance, migrating your client database and sphere, connecting website forms and Meta Lead Ads, syncing every team member's Google Calendar, building your follow-up cadences, and training the team. Live in two weeks — not two months.
