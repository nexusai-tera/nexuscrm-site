Where does a fence company's margin actually go?

Into the calls you couldn't answer in April, the lumber that went up after you quoted, and the shared back line nobody wrote down properly. Fencing is high-volume, seasonal, and priced by the foot — small leaks add up fast.

01

The spring rush you can't pick up

It's April. Everyone wants a new fence before the dog gets out or the pool opens. Your phone rings all day while you're setting posts, the voicemails stack up, and by the time you call back at 8 p.m. half of them have booked whoever answered first.

02

Lumber moved. Your quote didn't.

You priced a cedar privacy fence in March off last season's numbers. The customer signed in June. Cedar and posts went up in between, and nobody noticed until the supplier invoice landed — after the job was already booked at the old price.

03

Four neighbours, one fence, zero clarity

The back line is shared by four houses. One wants a gate, one wants the old chain link hauled away, one pays late. It lives in a notebook and three text threads, and at the end nobody — including you — knows what that fence actually made.

How the AI Caller Works

First call to final payment

What does a fence job look like on NexusCRM?

One system carries the job from the call you'd have missed to the last gate hung — with the margin known at every step.

The call gets answered

The AI Caller picks up at 7:48 p.m., qualifies the job, and the lead lands in NexusCRM with a summary and transcript. A web-form lead from your Meta ad gets called back within minutes instead of tomorrow.

Walk the line, talk the quote

A two-minute recording becomes a priced draft by the linear foot, with PT, cedar, and vinyl as options. The Bid Advisor checks the margin against today's price sheet and similar fences you've already built.

Customer taps Accept — the chain fires

The signed snapshot locks with timestamp and IP, and the invoice, project, and schedule entry are created automatically. Not signed yet? Expiry reminders go out, and the AI Caller can follow up by phone.

Locates, posts, then panels

The post-day task stays Blocked until locates are back. Post day and panel day are booked as separate visits, synced to Google Calendar, with day-before reminders so the crew shows up to the right yard.

Costs land as they happen

Per-foot crew pay, photographed lumber and hardware receipts, and disposal fees hit the live P&L the day they occur. Shared fences roll up to the project group without double-counting.

Final payment with a known margin

After photos go into the customer portal, the warranty is already on the signed quote, and paid-in-full emails the receipt. You close the fence knowing exactly what it made.

Fencing contractor CRM questions, answered

NexusCRM is flat-priced in CAD with unlimited users: Core is $295/month plus $2,950 one-time implementation, Pro is $425/month plus $5,500 (the fit for most fence companies running install crews), and Elite is $795/month plus $9,800. Annual prepay earns two months free, financed implementation is available, and the AI Caller for inbound, outbound and follow-up calls is an optional add-on from $297/month.

Can NexusCRM quote fences by the linear foot with different materials?

Yes. Quotes are multi-option, so one quote can price the same 150 linear feet in pressure-treated, cedar, and vinyl as Good, Better, and Best, with gates, removal and disposal, and hardware as their own lines. The customer ticks the option they want on their phone with a live running total and signs; billing charges exactly what was accepted. Voice-to-quote drafts those lines from your own past fence quotes and warns you when a line is priced under your historical low.

How do I handle neighbours splitting the cost of a shared fence?

Use a project group. The shared boundary run sits on the group, each neighbour keeps their own extras — a gate on one side, removal of an old fence on the other — and each signs and pays for their own portion. Payments and expenses are tracked at the group level without double-counting a dollar, and the group shows the true combined profitability of the whole fence, so a back line shared by four households is one job to you and a clear bill to each of them.

Can I pay fence install crews by the linear foot?

Yes. Per-linear-foot is one of NexusCRM's four crew pay models, alongside hourly, per-day, and per-project lump sum. The rate is locked when the crew is assigned, so a rate change next season never rewrites what an old job cost. Crew and subcontractor ledgers track what each installer has earned and been paid, crews can check what they are owed themselves, and every pay line lands on that job's live P&L.

What happens to my fence quotes when lumber prices jump?

You see it before it costs you. The materials price book keeps price history, and supplier PDF price lists can be imported by AI straight into catalog rows. The AI Bid Advisor shows line-by-line variance against your current price sheets before you send, and quotes carry automatic expiry — the customer gets reminders three days before and on the day, then the quote expires — so a March price does not get signed in June.

Can the AI Caller answer my phone during the spring rush?

Yes — that is what it is for. The AI Caller is a NexusAI add-on, from $297/month, built on NLPearl voice agents and wired to NexusCRM through n8n. It answers your line 24/7, asks the questions you would ask — address, rough footage, material, gates, removal, timeline — and transfers the call to you when a caller wants a person. When the call ends, the lead lands in NexusCRM with a summary and transcript, tagged AI Caller. It also calls new web and Meta leads within minutes and follows up on quotes that have not been accepted. It identifies itself as an AI assistant, and outbound calls only go to people who asked you for a quote.
