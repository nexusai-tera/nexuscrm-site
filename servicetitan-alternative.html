The sticker shock

How much does ServiceTitan actually cost per month?

Verified user reports from August 2026 put ServiceTitan at $245–$500 USD per technician per month — pricing is quote-only, so ServiceTitan itself won't tell you until you're on a sales call. Add a $5,000–$15,000+ implementation fee, a 12-month contract, and AI features sold separately as paid Pro add-ons.

The per-technician meter is what catches small shops. ServiceTitan's model was designed for operations with dozens of techs, dispatchers, and CSRs — so every tech you hire raises your software bill by $245–$500 USD a month. A 6-tech shop lands at $1,500–$3,000 USD monthly before implementation and before any Pro add-ons.

And those are US dollars. At 2026 exchange rates, Canadian shops pay roughly 35% more in CAD — turning a "mid-range" ServiceTitan quote into a $30,000–$68,900 CAD first-year commitment. NexusCRM's published pricing is CAD, flat, and doesn't move when you hire. For the wider market picture, see our full platform comparison.

The affordable-depth play

What does NexusCRM match — and what does it honestly not?

NexusCRM matches ServiceTitan's job-economics core — live per-job P&L, payroll and commissions, and data-grounded bid pricing — at about one-fifth of a 6-tech shop's year-one cost; it does not match ServiceTitan's enterprise dispatch and call-centre layer, and several conveniences are still on our roadmap.

Matched — at 1/5 the cost

  • Live job P&L — revenue, labour, materials, expenses and recoverable HST per job, updating as work happens, with 4 crew pay models
  • Payroll & commissions — crew and subcontractor ledgers, rep commissions, self-serve "what am I owed"
  • AI Bid Advisor — checks any quote against your price sheets and up to 25 similar completed jobs; ServiceTitan sells AI as paid Pro add-ons
  • Voice-to-quote — record a site walk, get a priced draft quote grounded in your own historical quotes
  • Quote-to-cash automation — customer accepts online and the invoice, project, schedule and crew notifications happen with zero clicks
  • Customer portal + e-acceptance, Claude-vision receipt capture, Google Calendar sync, mobile field views, 4-role access control with an append-only audit log

Not matched — the honest list

  • No enterprise dispatch board — NexusCRM schedules crews and jobs well, but there's no capacity-planned, dispatcher-grade board
  • No call-centre tooling — no inbound call booking, recording, or CSR scorecards
  • SMS & online card payments — roadmap only; ServiceTitan has both today
  • QuickBooks/Xero sync — roadmap only; today it's CSV exports for your bookkeeper
  • Memberships, recurring jobs, GPS time clock, customer self-booking — all roadmap, not shipping
  • No consumer financing at the point of sale

Straight answers

ServiceTitan alternative FAQ

How much does ServiceTitan cost per month?

ServiceTitan doesn't publish pricing — it's quote-only. Verified user reports from August 2026 put it at $245–$500 USD per technician per month, plus a $5,000–$15,000 implementation fee and a 12-month contract, with AI features sold as paid Pro add-ons. For a 6-technician shop that works out to roughly $1,500–$3,000 USD in software every month.

Is there a cheaper ServiceTitan alternative in Canada?

Yes. NexusCRM is built and supported in Toronto and costs a flat $425/month CAD (Pro) plus $5,500 one-time implementation, with unlimited users, all AI included, and Canadian hosting available. A 6-tech shop's year one is about $10,600 CAD on NexusCRM versus roughly $30,500–$68,900 CAD on ServiceTitan once USD pricing is converted at 2026 exchange rates.

What does ServiceTitan do better than NexusCRM?

Real enterprise depth. ServiceTitan has a true dispatch board with capacity planning, call-centre tooling with call recording, memberships and service agreements, consumer financing, integrated payments and texting today, and multi-location franchise reporting. If you run 15+ technicians with dedicated dispatchers and CSRs, ServiceTitan is built for you — and NexusCRM is not.

Does NexusCRM have SMS and online payments like ServiceTitan?

Not yet — to be straight with you: SMS text messaging, online card payments, and QuickBooks sync are roadmap items, not shipping features. ServiceTitan has them today. What NexusCRM ships today is email automation with open and click tracking, online quote e-acceptance, payment schedules on invoices, and webhook integrations with Zapier and n8n.

How long does it take to switch from ServiceTitan to NexusCRM?

About two weeks for most businesses. Implementation includes provisioning your private instance, migrating your customers and price book, applying your branding to quotes, invoices and the customer portal, connecting Google Calendar and lead sources, and training your team — all covered by the one-time implementation fee.

Does ServiceTitan require a long-term contract?

Yes — ServiceTitan's standard agreement is a 12-month contract, and pricing is quote-only. NexusCRM also has a 12-month initial term, but it then rolls month-to-month, pricing is published on our pricing page, and annual prepay earns two months free. Neither platform is a no-commitment product; the difference is what the commitment costs.
