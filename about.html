About NexusCRM

Built inside a working contractor

NexusCRM wasn't imagined in a boardroom. It was built inside Reformity Inc., a GTA contracting company, and it ran that company's daily operations before it was ever offered for sale.

The founder

Shaheen Rashidi

Founder of Reformity Inc. and NexusCRM.

Shaheen founded Reformity Inc. in 2018 to bring transparency, accountability and modern tools to GTA contracting.

NexusCRM grew out of that work. Quoting, scheduling, job costing and crew pay were built to run Reformity's own jobs first, so the product follows how a crew actually works.

NexusCRM is sold and supported in Toronto by NexusAI GTA.

Reformity Inc.

The company NexusCRM was built to run

Reformity is a North York-based general contractor serving the Greater Toronto Area across residential, commercial and industrial work. It started in fencing and landscaping.
