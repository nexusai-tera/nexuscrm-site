The challenge

As the business expanded, so did the admin: customer inquiries, estimates, follow-ups, scheduling, project coordination and expense management all needed attention.

Estimates were especially slow, because every project involved different materials, dimensions, labour and customer expectations. Meanwhile, responding quickly was critical to winning contracts.

The solution

NexusCRM brought customer management, estimating, sales follow-ups, project organization and financial oversight into one connected platform, with automation and AI-assisted workflows handling the repetitive work.

90% FASTER ESTIMATING

AI-assisted estimates
