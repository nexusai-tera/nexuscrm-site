Free trial and launch guarantee

What you get, what you don't, and what happens next, in plain language.

Book a Demo to Start

Free 14-day trial

Try it free for 14 days on a real job

No card. Book a demo to start. The goal is simple: send one real quote to one real customer.

What you get

  • A private instance with Pro features
  • Unlimited users
  • Your logo and your price sheet
  • Up to 20 of your past quotes loaded in

Who it's for

Crews of 3 or more, after the 25-minute demo. We run at most 3 trials at a time, so you may wait for a spot.

What isn't part of the trial

Full data migration, payroll setup, custom work and the AI Caller.

How it ends

The trial runs 14 days, with one 7-day extension if weather stops your site visits. Then your instance is paused and your data is kept for 30 days and deleted unless you sign. If you sign, normal published pricing and the 12-month initial term apply.

30-day launch guarantee

If it doesn't work on your data, you're not stuck with it

If what we showed you doesn't work on your data and we can't fix it in 14 days, you get your implementation fee back and you're out of the contract.

We sign a checklist at kickoff

The written checklist is what the guarantee measures. It runs for 30 days after go-live.

You tell us what's failing

Send written notice to info@nexusaigta.ca within 30 days of go-live.

We get 14 days to fix it

The 14 days start when we receive your notice.

If it still fails, you're out

We refund your implementation fee, cancel any remaining implementation financing, release you from the term and export your data to you.

What the checklist covers

  • Your data is imported and your team is trained
  • A quote can be built from your own pricing
  • Acceptance creates the invoice and the job
  • The job appears on the schedule and in Google Calendar
  • Labour and receipts land on the job P&L (Pro and up)

What isn't refunded

Monthly fees. On Elite, the refund is capped at $5,500 and custom development isn't refundable.

What it doesn't cover

A change of mind, roadmap items such as SMS, card payments and QuickBooks sync, third-party outages, and data you never supplied.

Prices in CAD. Full pricing is on the pricing page.
