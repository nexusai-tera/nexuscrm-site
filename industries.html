The universal pipeline

Why does one platform work across trades?

Because every service business — whatever the trade — runs the same five-stage pipeline: quote the work, get it accepted, schedule the people, invoice the customer, and track the profit. What changes per industry is your price book, templates, and pay models — and NexusCRM tunes all of that to you.

Quote

Record a site walk or client call and AI drafts a priced quote grounded in your own historical quotes — interlock by the square foot for a landscaper, fixtures and rough-ins for a plumber. The Bid Advisor checks your margin against price sheets and up to 25 similar completed jobs.

Accept

Customers open a branded quote page on their phone and accept with a tap. Signed snapshots are locked immutably with timestamp — the same e-acceptance flow whether you sell patios, panel upgrades, or engagement letters.

Schedule

Acceptance drops the job onto the calendar automatically. Crews, technicians, showings, or client meetings sync to Google Calendar across multiple accounts, with day-before reminders sent for you.

Invoice

Zero clicks: the invoice is created the moment the quote is accepted, with payment schedules and HST handled natively. The customer gets their portal link by email — no retyping, no dropped handoffs.

Profit

Hours, materials, and photographed receipts land on the live per-job P&L as work happens, with four crew pay models plus commissions feeding payroll. You know the real margin before the job closes.
