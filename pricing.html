Pricing questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does NexusCRM cost per month?

A flat $295/month (Core), $425/month (Pro), or $795/month (Elite) in CAD, plus one-time implementation of $2,950, $5,500, or $9,800 respectively. Every tier includes unlimited users and all AI features — there are no per-user fees.

What does the implementation fee cover?

Provisioning your dedicated private instance, your branding on quotes/invoices/emails/portal, migrating clients, leads and your price book, configuring quote templates and workflows, connecting Google Calendar and lead sources, and training your team — plus 30 days of launch support.

Are there per-user fees?

No. Unlimited users on every tier. Competitors typically charge $29–$35 per extra user monthly — ServiceTitan charges $245–$500 per technician.

Can I finance the implementation fee?

Yes — on Pro, pay $249/month over 24 months added to your subscription instead of $5,500 up front.

What are the contract terms?

12-month initial term, then month-to-month. Annual prepay earns two months free. You own your data and can export it in full at any time.

Is NexusCRM cheaper than ServiceTitan?

Substantially. ServiceTitan runs $245–$500 per technician per month plus a $5,000–$15,000+ implementation — a 6-tech shop can pay $1,500–$3,000 monthly. NexusCRM Pro is $425/month flat for your whole team, with AI included rather than sold as add-ons.
