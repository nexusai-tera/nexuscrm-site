Straight answers

Partner program FAQ

How and when do partners get paid?

Commissions are calculated on collected revenue and paid out monthly. Referral partners earn 15% of the implementation fee plus 20% of monthly recurring revenue for the client's first 12 months, with a 90-day clawback if the client cancels early. Certified Resellers earn 30% of setup plus 25% of MRR for the lifetime of the account. White-Label partners don't wait on payouts at all — you bill your clients directly at your own price and pay NexusAI GTA wholesale rates.

Who owns the client relationship?

It depends on your track. On the Referral track, you make the introduction and NexusAI GTA sells, bills, and supports the client. On the Certified Reseller track, you sell and own the relationship while NexusAI GTA handles billing and delivery. On the White-Label track the client is yours completely — your brand, your contract, your invoice, your price.

Are partner territories exclusive?

No — NexusCRM does not sell exclusive territories. Instead, deal registration protects every opportunity you bring: once a prospect is registered to you, the commission on that client is yours and no other partner can claim it. Registrations are per-client and first-come, anywhere in Canada.

How much support work lands on the partner?

On the Referral and Certified Reseller tracks, none — NexusAI GTA implements, trains, and supports every client directly. White-Label partners own first-line (L1) support under their own brand, which is why that track requires certification and three active clients first. NexusAI GTA maintains the platform, hosting, and upgrades behind you and takes escalations.

How deep does the white-label actually go?

The commercial layer is fully yours: you set the retail price, sign the contract, send the invoices, and deliver first-line support under your brand. NexusAI GTA provisions and maintains each client's dedicated private instance at wholesale rates — $2,995 setup plus $235/month CAD. Inside the product, quotes, invoices, and the customer portal carry the end client's own branding, so their customers see their company.

What are the requirements to join each track?

The Referral track has no requirements beyond registering your deals — anyone with an audience of service businesses can start today. The Certified Reseller track requires completing certification and comes with your own demo instance. The White-Label track requires certification plus three active clients before moving to wholesale billing.
