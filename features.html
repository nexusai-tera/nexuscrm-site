By architecture, not by policy document: every client runs on a dedicated private instance with its own database, four server-enforced roles, and an audit trail on every change — human or AI.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

What features are included in NexusCRM?

All of them, at a flat price. Every tier includes the full pipeline — lead capture, quotes, online e-signature acceptance, invoicing, scheduling with Google Calendar sync, and the customer portal — with unlimited users and AI included. Pro adds voice-to-quote, the AI Bid Advisor, receipt capture, live job costing, payroll, and commissions. There are no per-user fees and no AI add-on charges. See the pricing page for the tier-by-tier breakdown.

What can the AI assistant actually do?

The assistant has roughly 73 permission-aware tools: it can look up leads, quotes, invoices, and jobs, draft quotes, record payments, log hours and expenses, book or reschedule meetings, and run follow-ups. Anything destructive is staged with a plain-English preview you confirm before it executes, every tool call respects your role-based permissions, and every AI action lands in the audit log with a before/after diff.

Does NexusCRM have job costing?

Yes — a live per-job profit and loss is core to the product. It combines invoice revenue, labour computed from logged hours times pay rates snapshotted at crew assignment (hourly, daily, per-project, or per-linear-foot), categorized expenses with photographed receipts, and recoverable HST, then shows margin against your target while the job is still open. Project groups cost shared job sites correctly across multiple customers.

Can customers accept quotes online?

Yes. Customers open a branded, tokenized quote page on their phone — no login — pick the options they want, and approve with a typed e-signature. Acceptance is stored as an immutable signed snapshot with IP address and timestamp, and it automatically creates the invoice and the project, emails the customer their private portal link, and notifies your team.

Does NexusCRM work on phones in the field?

Yes. NexusCRM installs as a mobile PWA. Reps get a Today view with tap-to-call, tap-to-navigate, and a one-tap voice recorder for site walks; crews get their own task board with one-tap statuses and hours logging; and every major list in the app has a mobile card view. Crews only ever see the jobs assigned to them, with financials hidden.

Does NexusCRM integrate with QuickBooks or send SMS?

QuickBooks Online sync, SMS text messaging, and online card payments are the three headline items on the current roadmap. Today, NexusCRM covers email automation with open and click tracking, CSV exports for your bookkeeper, payment schedules on invoices, and signed webhook integrations with Zapier and n8n.
