Frequently asked questions

How fast will you reply to a demo request?

Within one business day. A human from the Toronto team replies with proposed demo times — there's no automated sales sequence and no pressure follow-up.

How long is the demo and what does it cover?

About 25 minutes. We map your current lead-to-invoice flow, walk it live on NexusCRM — including the AI voice-to-quote — and finish with straight talk on pricing, timeline, and whether it's actually a fit. See what the product does first if you'd like.

How much does NexusCRM cost?

Flat monthly pricing in CAD with unlimited users: Core is $295/month plus $2,950 one-time implementation, Pro is $425/month plus $5,500, and Elite is $795/month plus $9,800. All AI features are included in every tier — the full breakdown is published on the pricing page.

Do I need to prepare anything for the demo?

No preparation is required. If you want the most out of it, have a rough idea of your team size, a recent quote you sent, and the tools you currently use — we'll show your workflow running on NexusCRM. More answers on the FAQ page.

Can we talk about the partner program instead?

Yes. Agencies, consultants, and bookkeepers can email hello@nexusaigta.ca with the subject line PARTNER, or read the partner program page first for the referral, certified reseller, and white-label tracks.
