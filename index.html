Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

What is NexusCRM?

NexusCRM is an AI-powered CRM and field operations platform for service businesses — contractors, landscapers, plumbers, electricians, HVAC, real estate agents, and accountants. It runs your full pipeline from lead intake through quotes, online acceptance, invoicing, crew scheduling, job profitability, and payroll, with AI built into every step. It's built and supported in Toronto by NexusAI GTA.

How much does NexusCRM cost?

Flat monthly pricing with unlimited users: Core is $295/month + $2,950 one-time implementation, Pro is $425/month + $5,500 implementation, Elite is $795/month + $9,800 implementation — all CAD. No per-user fees. AI is built into every plan. Voice-to-quote, Bid Advisor and receipt capture start on Pro. AI Caller is a separate add-on, billed by talk minute. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Is my data private and hosted in Canada?

Yes. Every client gets a dedicated private instance with its own database — never pooled with other companies. Canadian hosting is available, supporting PIPEDA-aligned data-residency requirements, with role-based access control and an audit trail on every change.

What can the AI actually do?

Record a site walk and get a priced draft quote built from your own historical quotes. Photograph receipts into itemized job expenses. Run the Bid Advisor to check a quote's margin against similar completed jobs. Ask the AI assistant to look up or update records — with your confirmation and a full audit log on every action.

How long does it take to go live?

About two weeks for most businesses. Implementation covers your private instance, your branding on quotes/invoices/portal, data migration, calendar and lead-source integrations, and team training. Live in two weeks — not two months.

Does it integrate with QuickBooks? Can it text customers?

QuickBooks Online sync, SMS text messaging, and online card payments are on our roadmap but not live yet. Today you get email automation with open/click tracking, CSV exports for your bookkeeper, payment schedules on invoices, and webhook integrations with Zapier and n8n.
