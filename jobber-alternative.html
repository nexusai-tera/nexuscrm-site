Jobber alternative FAQ

Is NexusCRM cheaper than Jobber for a team?

For teams of about four or more, usually yes. Jobber's Grow plan runs $199–$499 USD/month plus $29 USD per additional user, and USD pricing costs Canadian businesses roughly 35% more at 2026 exchange rates. NexusCRM Pro is a flat $425/month CAD with unlimited users — a 6-person crew and a 12-person crew pay exactly the same. For a solo operator, Jobber's $49 Core plan is cheaper; NexusCRM's economics win as the crew grows. Full breakdown on the pricing page.

Can I import my Jobber data?

Yes. Migrating your Jobber data — clients, jobs, and your price book — is included in the $5,500 Pro implementation. We handle the export, field mapping, and cleanup, apply your branding to quotes, invoices and the customer portal, connect Google Calendar and your lead sources, and train your team. Most businesses are live in about two weeks.

Does NexusCRM have SMS like Jobber?

Not yet — and we say so plainly. Jobber ships mature two-way SMS today, and it is one of Jobber's genuine strengths. SMS is on NexusCRM's active roadmap alongside online card payments and QuickBooks Online sync. Today NexusCRM handles customer communication through automated email with open and click tracking, the customer portal, and day-before reminders. If SMS is mission-critical for you right now, Jobber is stronger on that point.

What are NexusCRM's contract terms?

NexusCRM has a 12-month initial term, then goes month-to-month. Annual prepay earns two months free, and the Pro implementation can be financed at $249/month over 24 months. Jobber's Core plan is month-to-month from day one — genuinely more flexible if zero commitment matters most to you. We ask for 12 months because white-glove implementation is front-loaded work.

What does Jobber cost in CAD?

Jobber prices in USD: Core is $49/month and Grow runs $199–$499/month plus $29 per additional user, per Jobber's public pricing as of August 2026. At 2026 exchange rates, Canadian businesses pay roughly 35% more in CAD — a $299 USD software bill lands around $405 CAD before extra seats. NexusCRM is priced flat in CAD at $295, $425, or $795 per month, so there is no exchange-rate creep.

Why is flat pricing better for crews?

Because crews grow in the field, not in the office. On per-user pricing, every new hire is also a software decision — at $29 USD per seat, four extra crew members add roughly $1,900 CAD a year before they have billed an hour. Flat pricing with unlimited users means everyone gets a login — crew leads, subs, your bookkeeper — so schedules, hours, and job costs are actually complete.
